Build log

Build log: what runs on testnet today

Dated entries on what LEXI runs on Base Sepolia (testnet), what is in progress and what is next. Each entry carries a date, a label and an author, and says why anything is delayed. Newest first.

Nothing is live on mainnet. Every entry on this page describes Base Sepolia (testnet) unless it says otherwise.

Page last reviewed: 2026-10-16

What runs today

LEXI's V0 prototype runs end to end on Base Sepolia, Base's public testnet (chain ID 84532): a protocol submits documentation, the LEXI engine produces a first-pass work product, a co-signature flow runs, and a credential is recorded on-chain as a 32-byte credential id with hash commitments. The flow was confirmed end to end on 2026-08-04. Nothing is live on Base mainnet; mainnet is targeted post-audit, Q4 2026, VARA first.

2026-10-10 · PlannedLex Arbitrage

Mainnet target moved to post-audit, Q4 2026

Mainnet is now targeted post-audit, Q4 2026, VARA first. The earlier public target was a fixed week in late October 2026. Hardening and a security audit come before any mainnet deployment, and no auditor is engaged yet, so the date now follows the audit rather than the calendar.

The status line on every page reads: PRE-LAUNCH · V0 LIVE ON TESTNET · MAINNET TARGETED POST-AUDIT, Q4 2026. Nothing is live on mainnet, and this page will say so until it is.

2026-09-24 · DecisionLex Arbitrage

Co-signers are licensed lawyers only

From this date a legal professional on LEXI means a licensed lawyer. Compliance officers do not stake and do not co-sign, and no page on this site lists them as a co-signer type. They still appear where they belong: as the people at exchanges and other reliers who read credentials.

One assigned licensed lawyer co-signs each credential. No AI signs anything. What a co-signing lawyer signs, stakes and is exposed to is on the page for legal professionals.

2026-09-08 · DecisionLex Arbitrage

Roadmap set: V0 VARA on testnet, V1 the LEXI Agent with MiCA and FIU-IND, V2 connectors and an API

The roadmap now has three versions. V0, on testnet: opinion issuance as an on-chain credential, VARA (Dubai) the base jurisdiction. V1, next up: a refreshed LEXI Agent built for long-horizon diligence, with persistent memory and iterative agentic search across many rulebooks, plus new jurisdictions starting with the EU (MiCA) and India (FIU-IND), so one opinion can span several. V2, after V1: agent connectors (DocuSign, GitHub) to cross-check protocol-asserted facts, and a standalone LEXI Verification Service API for enterprise compliance and KYB. US coverage is on the roadmap.

The same three versions appear on the key facts page, with the maturity of each.

In progress

Hardening, then the audit.

Hardening

The V0 flow is being hardened before any mainnet deployment. Protocols, firms and signatories sign their own transactions in the client; an EU-hosted backend relays them and owns gas, nonces and retries. The field set on the credential page is the V0 target schema.

The audit

Contracts are audited before mainnet. No auditor is engaged yet. Mainnet follows the audit, not the other way round, and this log will carry the engagement as an entry when it happens.

Mainnet, post-audit

Targeted Q4 2026, VARA (Dubai) first. USDC price points publish at mainnet. Until then every credential on this site is a specimen or a testnet record, and there is nothing on mainnet to rely on.

LEXI.

LEXI Protocol is compliance infrastructure for crypto on the India, UAE and US corridor. It turns a licensed lawyer's co-signed legal opinion into a dated, scope-bound credential on Base that the parties it names can verify in one call. VARA first, running on Base Sepolia testnet today; the EU (MiCA) and India (FIU-IND) are V1, next up. Mainnet is targeted post-audit, Q4 2026.
