The corridor

Three rulebooks that do not talk to each other.

A project touching India, the UAE and the US has to satisfy three regulators. Counsel in sequence takes 8 to 12 weeks and ends in a PDF nobody downstream can verify. LEXI clears one jurisdiction at a time, and clearing one means obtaining a co-signed legal opinion covering it, recorded as a credential the named parties check themselves.

Fig. 01 The corridor. One credential per cleared jurisdiction; the line leaves through the gate, VARA first. MiCA (EU) joins FIU-IND in V1.

How it works

From documents to credential, in one flow.

  1. 01

    Submit once

    Upload documentation to encrypted storage and pick the jurisdictions you need.

  2. 02

    The engine runs the first pass

    Checklist, document review, risk read. The output is work product, not an opinion. No AI signs anything.

  3. 03

    A licensed lawyer takes over

    They review the work product, run their own diligence, and co-sign the legal opinion with a stake behind the signature.

  4. 04

    The opinion becomes a credential

    Dated, scope-bound, naming its reliers. Hash commitments go to Base, and reliers verify in one call.

AI-assisted, lawyer co-signed legal opinions, recorded as verifiable credentials on Base; the flow runs on Base Sepolia testnet today. The LEXI engine is in development on a proprietary corpus of real regulatory work. One assigned licensed lawyer co-signs each credential.

The credential

One opinion, three layers of disclosure.

The on-chain record holds hashes and metadata. The opinion body stays off-chain, encrypted, open to the reliers the credential names. Non-transferable; the token standard is undecided. No risk score is written on-chain.

A LEXI credential is a co-signed legal opinion committed on-chain. It is not a licence, a registration, or an approval by any regulator. LEXI is not licensed or endorsed by VARA.

For exchanges, VASPs and Licensed Distributors

Read a credential in three steps.

  1. Status is Live, not Expired or Revoked.

    As-of date, expiry and revocation are on-chain.Does it still speak as of a date I accept?

  2. The scope and opinion hashes match the documents you hold.

    Hash the PDF and the scope text and compare them with the on-chain commitments.Is this the document the lawyer signed?

  3. Your entity is a named relier and has accepted the reliance terms.

    The credential names who may rely on it; the reliance record shows you accepted the terms.Am I expressly authorised to rely?
