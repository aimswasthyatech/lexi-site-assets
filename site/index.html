What LEXI is

A legal opinion you can rely on, recorded where anyone can check it.

LEXI Protocol is compliance infrastructure that turns co-signed legal opinions into dated, scope-bound, verifiable on-chain credentials for Web3 teams operating across India, the UAE and the US. Built on Base.

A fine-tuned engine prepares the first pass as work product. A licensed legal professional runs their own diligence and co-signs the opinion. The credential names who may rely on it, and those reliers can verify the credential on-chain in a single call. To be clear about the words: "clearing" a jurisdiction on LEXI means obtaining a co-signed legal opinion covering it. A credential is not a licence, a registration, or a regulator's approval.

The problem

Web3 is borderless. Regulation isn't.

A project touching India, the UAE and the US has to satisfy frameworks that do not talk to each other. The UAE alone added a federal layer when the Capital Market Authority's decree-laws came into force in January 2026.

How it works

From documents to credential, in one flow.

  1. 01

    Submit once

    Upload documentation to encrypted storage and pick the jurisdictions you need.

  2. 02

    The engine runs the first pass

    Checklist, document review, risk read. Its output is work product, not an opinion. No AI signs anything at LEXI.

  3. 03

    A licensed professional takes over

    They review the work product, run their own diligence, and co-sign the legal opinion with a stake behind their signature.

  4. 04

    The opinion becomes a credential

    Dated, scope-bound, naming its reliers. Hash commitments go to Base; reliers verify in one call.

The protocol

Three layers. One credential.

An AI does the reading, a licensed human owns the judgment, and the result lives on-chain where anyone can check it.

01

Regulatory intelligence engineThe AI reads the rulebooks

A fine-tuned engine, in development on a proprietary corpus of real regulatory work, produces the checklist, the document review and the risk read. All of it is work product. Anything below confidence routes to the human who owns the opinion.

02

Legal professionalsA licensed human signs it

Licensed lawyers post a USD-pegged stake of about $5,000 in $LEXI to be eligible to co-sign. Their on-chain record is their track record. When a relier flags a dispute, a committee adjudicates it and can slash the stake for proven misconduct.

03

On-chain attestation layerThe credential anyone can verify

A credential on Base that commits to the opinion by hash and names who may rely on it. The opinion body stays off-chain, encrypted. A credential only mints against the signatory's own signature, and signing keys never leave the signatory. Individual zkKYC is a later pillar.

$LEXI

The token is plumbing, not the product.

No token is live and nothing on this site is an offer or solicitation. The mechanics below are protocol design.

Pricing

Services in USDC

Protocols and reliers pay in USDC for credentials and reliance access. Customers never need to hold $LEXI.

Mechanism

Buyback and burn

40% of protocol USDC revenue is used to buy $LEXI on-market and burn it. 60% funds the treasury and operations.

Staking

The signatory bond

Legal professionals stake a USD-pegged $5,000 in $LEXI to be eligible to co-sign. Slashing is committee-adjudicated.

Governance

Parameters, not verdicts

Holders vote on jurisdictions, fees and treasury. Token holders never vote on whether a protocol is compliant.

Roadmap

Now, next, later.

Now · live on testnet

V0 · VARA first

V0 is live on Base Sepolia (testnet), end to end: documentation in, engine first pass, co-signature flow, credential minted on testnet. VARA is the base jurisdiction. Hardening and the mainnet audit are next. Mainnet target: the fourth week of October 2026.

Next up

V1 · LEXI Agent and new jurisdictions

A fully refreshed LEXI Agent, the engine behind the first pass, rebuilt around long-horizon diligence work with persistent agent memory, and native iterative agentic search across hundreds or thousands of rulebooks in multiple jurisdictions. New jurisdictions, starting with the EU (MiCA) and India (FIU-IND). One opinion can then span multiple jurisdictions.

After V1

V2 · Connectors and a verification API

Agent connectors, so the LEXI Agent can cross-verify protocol-asserted facts against external tools such as DocuSign and GitHub. A standalone LEXI Verification Service, delivered as an API for enterprise compliance and KYB workflows: enterprises verify that a counterparty meets a minimum compliance threshold and gate their services accordingly.
