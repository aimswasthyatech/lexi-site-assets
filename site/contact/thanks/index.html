LEXI.

LEXI Protocol is compliance infrastructure for crypto on the India, UAE and US corridor. It turns a licensed lawyer's co-signed legal opinion into a dated, scope-bound credential on Base that the parties it names can verify in one call. VARA first, running on Base Sepolia testnet today; the EU (MiCA) and India (FIU-IND) are V1, next up. Mainnet is targeted post-audit, Q4 2026.

Nothing on this site is legal advice. LEXI is not a law firm, and no attorney-client relationship arises from using this site. Legal opinions are issued by independent licensed professionals to named reliers under a defined scope.

$LEXI is designed as the protocol's staking and governance asset. No token is currently offered for sale, and nothing on this site is an offer of securities or a solicitation to invest, in any jurisdiction. Virtual assets carry risk, including the loss of their entire value.

A LEXI credential is a co-signed legal opinion committed on-chain. It is not a licence, a registration, or an approval by any regulator.
