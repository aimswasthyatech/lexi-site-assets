# Pricing: LEXI Protocol

Last updated: 2026-09-24

## Model

- All clearance services are priced in USDC, scoped per jurisdiction set. Customers never need to hold $LEXI.
- One credential covers one cleared jurisdiction, where "cleared" means a licensed professional has co-signed a scoped legal opinion covering it.
- Reliers pay a fixed USDC fee for reliance access: a licence to the gated opinion body plus an on-chain reliance record. Reading public on-chain metadata is free.
- 40% of protocol USDC revenue funds a buyback-and-burn of $LEXI; 60% goes to the protocol treasury. The co-signing professional is not paid from protocol revenue; the protocol retains the firm separately.

## Status

- LEXI is pre-launch. V0 is live on Base Sepolia (testnet) with VARA (Dubai) as the base jurisdiction; mainnet is targeted for September to October 2026. USDC price points publish here at mainnet.
- Proof of concept on testnet: a modest USDC pilot fee under a short pilot agreement, credited in full toward the first mainnet credential.
- Early protocols can request a scoped quote now: info@lexiprotocol.com

## For legal professionals

- Eligibility stake: a USD-pegged $5,000 in $LEXI, operative at token launch.
- Slashing: committee-adjudicated, for proven misconduct only, capped; reliers flag disputes, they do not slash.
