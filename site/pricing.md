# Pricing: LEXI Protocol

Last updated: 2026-10-11

LEXI Protocol is not a law firm and gives no legal advice. Legal opinions are issued by independent licensed lawyers to named reliers under a defined scope.

## Model

- All clearance services are priced in USDC, scoped per jurisdiction set. Customers never need to hold $LEXI.
- One credential covers one cleared jurisdiction, where "cleared" means a licensed lawyer has co-signed a scoped legal opinion covering it. A LEXI credential is not a licence, a registration, or an approval by any regulator.
- Reading a credential's public on-chain metadata is free for anyone.
- Reliance access (a licence to the gated opinion body plus an on-chain reliance record) is a fixed USDC fee paid by the relier.
- 40% of protocol USDC revenue is used to acquire $LEXI on-market and retire it; 60% funds the treasury and operations. The co-signing lawyer is not paid from protocol revenue; the protocol retains the firm separately, outside LEXI.

## Status

- LEXI is pre-launch. V0 runs end to end on Base Sepolia (testnet) with VARA (Dubai) as the base jurisdiction; mainnet is targeted post-audit, Q4 2026. The EU (MiCA) and India (FIU-IND) are V1, next up.
- USDC price points publish here at mainnet. No fee, quote or pilot is offered before then, and nothing on testnet carries a charge.
- To talk to us: info@lexiprotocol.com

## For legal professionals

- Eligibility stake (protocol design): a USD-pegged amount of about $5,000 in $LEXI, re-pegged so the dollar cost stays stable, with a 14-day unbonding period. No $LEXI token has been issued.
- Slashing: committee-adjudicated, for proven misconduct only, capped in basis points; reliers flag disputes, they do not slash.
