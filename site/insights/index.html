How these briefings are made

The editorial line.

Editorial line applies to every briefing
AuthorEvery briefing is by Lex Arbitrage, Co-Founder and CEO, and carries that byline with a visible Published date.
SourcesEvery fact names its regulator or statutory source and the date it was checked at source. Law-firm summaries are named in a Sources block when used, never linked. Where a regulator has not published a text, the briefing says so instead of guessing.
DatesPublished and Last updated dates describe the page, not the regulation. A briefing is never re-dated without a substantive change. The tracker ships as numbered editions with a visible changes section.
FrameEach entry is a regulator event stated as a fact, with what it does to a legal opinion's scope and date. The briefings do not say whether a given project is licensable and do not describe licensing steps. That is a question for counsel.
ReferenceThe terms the briefings use (as-of date, named relier, reliance mode, scope hash, status) are defined in the glossary. What runs on testnet today is recorded, dated, in the build log.
These briefings are educational commentary, not legal advice, and each speaks as of its published date. LEXI is not a law firm. For questions about your own structure, obtain advice from licensed counsel in the relevant jurisdictions.

LEXI Protocol is compliance infrastructure for crypto on the India, UAE and US corridor.
