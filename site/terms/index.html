1. What this site is

lexiprotocol.com describes the LEXI protocol, which is pre-launch, with V0 live on Base Sepolia (testnet). Nothing is live on mainnet; mainnet is targeted post-audit, Q4 2026. Content is provided for information only. It may change as the protocol design moves through build, audit and governance, and the site says so where it matters.

2. No legal advice, no law firm

Nothing on this site is legal advice. LEXI is not a law firm and does not practice law. No attorney-client relationship arises from reading this site, contacting us, or using the protocol. Legal opinions referenced on this site are issued by independent licensed lawyers, engaged directly by their clients, to named reliers under a defined scope.

3. No offer, no solicitation

Nothing on this site is an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy any security, financial instrument or virtual asset, in any jurisdiction. No $LEXI token has been issued and no public sale is open. Any future token event will be conducted under its own terms and eligibility restrictions, which will exclude persons from jurisdictions where participation would be unlawful, including US persons in any public sale. The token's design role is documented at how $LEXI works.

4. Credentials and reliance

A LEXI credential records a co-signed legal opinion. It is not a licence, a registration, or an approval by any regulator. Reliance on an opinion is available only to the reliers the credential names, and only after those reliers accept the applicable reliance terms. Reading public credential metadata confers no reliance rights. What a credential records and how a relier checks it: the LEXI credential.

LEXI.

LEXI Protocol is compliance infrastructure for crypto on the India, UAE and US corridor. It turns a licensed lawyer's co-signed legal opinion into a dated, scope-bound credential on Base that the parties it names can verify in one call. VARA first, running on Base Sepolia testnet today; the EU (MiCA) and India (FIU-IND) are V1, next up. Mainnet is targeted post-audit, Q4 2026.
